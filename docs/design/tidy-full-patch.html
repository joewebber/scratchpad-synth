Filter

A filter turns part of the sound down. Set to low-pass, it keeps the lows and removes harmonics above the cutoff, so the sound gets darker.

What it does

Removes harmonics above the cutoff frequency. Resonance adds a peak right at the cutoff.

Listen for

Hold a note and drag Cutoff down. The brightness drains out.

Try this

Switch the scope to Filter and Spectrum. The bars on the right go first.

Your patch

Two saws, the second 7 cents sharp, and a sine two octaves down, through a hard-clipped 24 dB low-pass at 2 kHz. A filter envelope opens it and a snappy amp envelope shapes each note. LFO 1 wobbles the cutoff and speeds up LFO 2, which moves the resonance. Then a chorus.
